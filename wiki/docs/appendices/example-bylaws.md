# Example Bylaws

- A model governance charter that codifies the principles of a [Crisis Cadre](../cadres/what-is-a-cadre.md), intended as an adaptable template rather than legal advice.
- Articles cover name and purpose, membership, governance structure, meetings, committee management, decision-making, core principles, amendments, and dissolution.
- Admission requires two sponsors, a probationary period, joint vetting, and a two-thirds supermajority vote by secret ballot.
- Membership is open to those committed to the Cadre's purpose, with a standing obligation to reflect the diversity of its [bioregion](../culture/bioregional-design.md).
- The [Four Pillars](../pillars/index.md) organize all cadre work, with [subsidiarity](../governance/subsidiarity.md) empowering committees within their domains.
- Officers and committee chairs rotate annually via sortition and remain easily recallable.
- Democratic centralism, evidence as law, [political compartmentalization](../culture/compartmentalization.md), [bioregionalism](../culture/bioregional-design.md), and [corporate camouflage](../security/corporate-edge.md) are codified as core principles.
- Amendments require a two-thirds supermajority; dissolution requires a three-fourths vote.
