# Education

- Education is the cadre's living mind: it asks what the group must know and ensures critical skills are deeply embedded, widely shared, and constantly evolving.
- Core responsibilities include knowledge preservation, conflict resolution, outreach, media relations, and [credentialing](credentialing.md).
- Education identifies skill gaps and designs tiered programs to fill them, in partnership with [Security](../security/index.md) for critical skills.
- Cadres should cultivate a reputation as extremely learned and experienced: able to suture wounds, plant corn, mediate conflict, and balance books.
- [Media relations](media-narratives.md) default to silence, and the cadre's story is told on its own terms through controlled channels.
- Sub-topics: [Education Fundamentals](fundamentals.md), [Media Narratives and Information Sovereignty](media-narratives.md), and [Essential Skills and Credentialing](credentialing.md).
