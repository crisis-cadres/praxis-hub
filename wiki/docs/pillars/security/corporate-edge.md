# The Corporate Edge

- Small businesses enjoy near-universal goodwill and avoid the ideological reactions triggered by explicitly political projects.
- A group that organizes as a business can exploit the blind spot of authoritarian systems that tolerate commerce while crushing dissent.
- The key lies in banality: the more ordinary the business, the more effectively it disappears into the economic landscape.
- A corporate framework provides built-in stability: bank accounts, contracts, supply chains, and durable legal existence.
- Structure acts as armor, outlasting individuals and [compartmentalizing operations](../strategy/compartmentalization.md) so no single member holds all threads.
- Preferred sectors include disaster recovery, waste management, agriculture, renewable energy, and medical supplies.
- Prefer an LLC or corporation over a co-op or non-profit, operating cooperatively through a private [operating agreement](bylaws.md) with temporary appointed figureheads.
