# Security Fundamentals

- Security begins with practical prevention: auditing spaces, installing lights, reinforcing locks, and delineating private and communal areas.
- Protect the products of [Production](../production/index.md) and [Procurement](../procurement/index.md) from theft, spoilage, and pests through locked storage and integrated pest management.
- Every member should have at least some security training to prevent a "warrior caste" from forming.
- Establish a clear chain of command in the field, even in otherwise horizontal organizations.
- Run scenario-based crisis simulations until responses are reflexive and fear is overridden by training.
- The goal is not a paranoid fortress but a culture of confidence through competence.
