# The Audit

- By analyzing receipts and purchase records, Procurement spots recurring expenditures and asks the critical question: could we produce this ourselves?
- Poll committees, individuals, and receipts to determine where the group's money is going.
- If vegetables come from distant suppliers, expand gardens or invest in greenhouses; if repairs drain funds, organize workshops to build those skills.
- Bring these insights to the wider community to democratize decisions on where to invest in local capacity.
- The audit shifts Procurement from a passive buyer to an architect of resilience, always seeking dual-win recommendations that lower costs while increasing resilience.
