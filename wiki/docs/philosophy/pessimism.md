# From Pessimism to Action

- Honest pessimism requires confronting reality before acting.
- Jem Bendell's 2018 paper "Deep Adaptation" argues [climate change is irreversible](realistic-pessimism.md) and near-term societal collapse is either likely, inevitable, or already unfolding, while critiquing sustainability efforts as insufficient.<sup>[\[1\]](#cite-1)</sup>
- Bendell was once dismissed as alarmist, but subsequent climate data and events have proven him largely correct, with many measures exceeding predictions.
- Even if the worst projections are wrong, preparedness pays off: if we are ready for the worst, we can face coming crises and help others.
- Maintaining a [realistic pessimistic outlook](realistic-pessimism.md) best informs rational decisions about the future.
