# Existential Dread

- Existential dread is not new, but today it is amplified by globalized risks: climate disasters, mass extinctions, AI, pandemics, political instability, and societal [collapse](when-empires-fall.md).<sup>[\[1\]](#cite-1)</sup>
- A 2021 global survey of 10,000 young people aged 16–25 across 10 countries found 75% said the future is frightening, and over half question whether having children is ethical.<sup>[\[1\]](#cite-1)</sup>
- "Solastalgia," emotional distress caused by environmental change, is a hidden toll of collapse.<sup>[\[2\]](#cite-2)</sup>
- Research shows not all eco-emotions are equal: anger is the most adaptive response, linked to better mental health and more climate action.<sup>[\[3\]](#cite-3)</sup>
- Anxiety and depression are less adaptive and often lead to disengagement.<sup>[\[3\]](#cite-3)</sup>
- The goal is to move from grief and anxiety to anger, and from anger to [collective action](cooperation.md).
