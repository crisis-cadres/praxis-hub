# Federation and Confederation

- Beyond [Dunbar's limit](../culture/dunbars-number.md), encourage the formation of new [semi-autonomous cadres](../cadres/what-is-a-cadre.md) rather than a monolithic bureaucracy.
- Cadres confederate through shared principles and protocols, mutual aid pacts, and common operational standards.
- Rotating delegates form a [council](../appendices/meeting-templates.md) for inter-cadre coordination, preventing the permanent centralization of power.
- Modular redundancy means the failure of one cadre does not collapse the network.
- This federated model mirrors the Iroquois Confederacy, certain anarchist collectives, and cooperative networks like Mondragon.
