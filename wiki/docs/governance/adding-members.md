# Adding Members

- Membership processes commonly include sponsorship, vetting, voting thresholds, and [probationary periods](../appendices/example-bylaws.md).
- Most elite groups require a sponsor, and many require two current members to nominate a candidate.
- Vetting may include background checks, interviews, and waiting periods, as with the Freemasons or Augusta National.
- Admission thresholds range from a simple majority to unanimous consent; many groups require a supermajority.
- Make it easier to remove a member than to admit one, echoing the principle of recallability.
- Probationary periods give prospective members a trial membership and a chance to contribute.
- Make prospective members feel welcome and [integrate them into active projects](../appendices/meeting-templates.md) immediately.
- [Diversity](diversity.md) is non-negotiable when adding members.
