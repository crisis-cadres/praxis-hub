# Governance Framework

- Representatives and authority figures should be recallable, with the threshold for recall lower than that of nomination.
- Distribute committee assignments evenly so no one is overloaded and no committee is empty.
- Sortition, or drawing lots, randomly assigns key roles and committee positions to prevent stratification.
- Democratic centralism: full and open debate before a decision, then unified support and implementation once decided.
- Give significant weight to the findings of specialized, evidence-based committees once ratified.
- Leadership becomes a temporary duty or service, not a path to personal power.
