# Bylaws and Operating Agreements

- Bylaws codify decision-making processes, voting thresholds, conflict resolution steps, leadership terms, and [recall procedures](framework.md).
- Bylaws can spell out [adding members](adding-members.md), handling departures, creating committees, and any other rules the group chooses.
- When in doubt, bylaws can state that meetings follow a specific edition of [Robert's Rules](../appendices/roberts-rules.md).
- Operating agreements are always considered private and need not be published, unlike bylaws in certain jurisdictions.
- Operating agreements are beneficial for cadres following [The Corporate Edge](../pillars/security/corporate-edge.md).
- Use concise editions of [Robert's Rules](../appendices/roberts-rules.md) and the [meeting templates](../appendices/meeting-templates.md) to keep procedures efficient.
- See the [Example Bylaws](../appendices/example-bylaws.md) appendix for a model charter adaptable to your cadre.
