# Praxis Hub

- Praxis Hub is the open-source home of the Crisis Cadres guide, operated on GitHub as a living document.
- The guide is a well-researched hypothesis to be tested, debated, and improved upon by those serious about doing the work.
- Anyone can contribute: fix typos, clarify sentences, correct errors, or suggest entirely new sections via pull requests.
- Field reports and case studies are especially valuable: tell us what worked and what failed spectacularly.
- The Code of Conduct keeps collaboration disciplined and mission-focused, with [political compartmentalization](../cadres/compartmentalization.md) and no personal attacks.
- The work is published under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International License.
- The project is a hypothesis that enough people are ready to turn shared anxiety into shared action.
