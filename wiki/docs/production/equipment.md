# Equipment Fabrication and Restoration

- A society built on disposable goods is perpetually at the mercy of those who sell them.
- A basic workshop, a place for repairs, is the logical first step: sewing machines mend torn fabric and soldering irons resurrect electronics.
- Beyond repair lies fabrication: blacksmiths and machinists turn scrap metal into usable parts, and 3D printers craft replacements locally.
- Where new materials are scarce, salvage becomes an art: old barrels become rainwater collectors, pallets become shelving, and broken appliances yield wire, gears, and motors.
- Very little is truly waste; think of it as raw material waiting to be reshaped.
