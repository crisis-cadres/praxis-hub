# Energy

* Energy is the backbone of every other pillar — food, shelter, and security all depend on reliable power.
* Groups should prioritize low-tech, repairable generation such as solar, wind, and micro-hydro over fragile, centralized infrastructure.
* Small-scale storage and efficiency habits reduce dependence on an unstable grid.
* Decentralized, redundant systems ensure that the failure of a single node cannot black out the community.
