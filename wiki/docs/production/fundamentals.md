# Production Fundamentals

- The ability to produce life's necessities is the bedrock of true independence.
- Production focuses relentlessly on essentials: food, water, shelter, energy, tools, and medicine, before anything else.
- Avoid financialized and detached activities, such as crypto schemes, influencer culture, and endless consulting engagements, that generate revenue but not survival value.
- The results of production must serve a clear, cadre-determined purpose rather than decoration.
- The goal is always the same: move from dependency to self-sufficiency, one step at a time.
