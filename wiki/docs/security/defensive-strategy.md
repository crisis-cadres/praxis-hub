# Defensive Strategy

- Study recent military conflicts without glorifying war, focusing strictly on the material factors behind success and failure.
- Cheap and plentiful consumer-grade drones have become the largest cause of battlefield casualties; countermeasures may become critical, depending on your position in the [crisis continuum](../cadres/crisis-continuum.md).
- Successful defensive strategies spread out resources, use earth-bermed or semi-subterranean structures, and manage the group's profile.
- Going at least partially underground offers dual wins: protection and low-cost thermal regulation against extreme heat.
- Deterrence comes from visible competence and organization, rather than posturing.
- Security should control the narrative and terrain in a high-threat scenario, but offensive conquest is always off the table.
