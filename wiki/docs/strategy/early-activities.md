# Early Activities and Shared Concerns

- Organize around [disaster preparedness](../cadres/crisis-continuum.md) with something easy like a neighborhood [call tree](../security/index.md) for redundant coverage.
- Ask the shared questions: do we have enough to eat if stores close, enough clean water, and support for the elderly and disabled?
- Schedule regular meetings and organize potlucks; [Procurement](../procurement/index.md) determines who brings what and [Production](../production/index.md) makes the meal.
- Form sub-committees for specific projects, such as a Horticulture sub-committee under [Production](../production/index.md) for a community garden.
- Potatoes are a great starter crop: resilient, calorie-rich, and difficult to mess up.
- Start now, since growing methods can take multiple seasons to perfect in your context.
- Stop worrying about when things will happen and act; shared needs are the organizing focus.
