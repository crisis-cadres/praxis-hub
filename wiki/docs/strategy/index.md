# Organizational Strategy and Growth

- Early organizing should begin with simple, low-risk collective projects, such as a [bulk staple food purchase](bulk-buy.md), to build trust and demonstrate material benefits.
- Groups must operate within [Dunbar's Number](dunbars-number.md), keeping functional units small to preserve deep social trust.
- When groups grow beyond human-scale cognitive limits, they should scale through [federation](federation.md) rather than centralized bureaucracy.
- [Diversity](diversity.md) is a strict strategic imperative that prevents groupthink and enhances problem-solving.
- [Starting a cadre](../cadres/what-is-a-cadre.md) means naming the concrete problem, finding a few committed people, and building trust through small wins.
