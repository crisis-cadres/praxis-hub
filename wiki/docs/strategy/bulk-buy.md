# Praxis 101: The Bulk Buy

- A single pooled purchase of a staple food is the repeatable first project that teaches the [Cadre model](../cadres/what-is-a-cadre.md) through action, not discussion.
- The proposal must be specific, low-risk, and offer an undeniable advantage over individual effort.
- Assign small teams for the functions of the [Four Pillars](../pillars/index.md): money and logistics, division and processing, communication, and storage.
- Keep the money simple so everyone contributes the same amount and the ledger is transparent.
- Conduct an [after-action review](../appendices/meeting-templates.md) with evidence-based questions before introducing any theory.
- No grounding in theory of any kind is required to get started; trust, cohesion, and competence are byproducts of the exercise.
