# Diversity as a Strategic Imperative

- Diversity is a pragmatic imperative for survival and antifragility, not tokenism or quotas.
- Diverse teams solve complex problems better through a broader range of perspectives and experience.
- A resilient cadre mirrors the demographics of its [bioregion](bioregional-design.md), or actively seeks global perspectives where local diversity is limited.
- Homogeneity must be justified and accompanied by a plan to correct the imbalance.
- Integration, not tokenism: distribute leadership, rotate decision-making power, and ensure all voices shape the [cadre's direction](../cadres/what-is-a-cadre.md).
- Think of diversity as risk management: a human polyculture thrives under stress where a monoculture fails.
