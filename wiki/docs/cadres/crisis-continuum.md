# The Crisis Continuum

- Cadres exist on a continuum, from minor inconveniences on one end to existential threats on the other.
- On the near end, [shared-nothing groups](cadre-types.md) handle routine disruptions such as wildfire smoke or brief power outages.
- On the far end, [fully committed cadres](cadre-types.md) manage dire events, with specialized training required and members engaged around the clock.
- Cadres adapt their systems to their particular circumstances; recommendations that sound extreme are not prescribed for every context.
- Degrees of Planning: with global temperatures already near 1.3°C above pre-industrial levels, warming thresholds define what resilience must look like.
- At 1+°C (we are here), harden defenses: passive cooling, fire-resistant landscaping, drought-resistant crops, and water capture.
- At 2°C, pursue radical self-sufficiency: greenhouses, community grain reserves, water as currency, and a possible retreat from coastlines.
- At 3°C, operate as disaster triage: controlled-environment agriculture, fortified settlements, and clustering in viable zones.
- At 4°C and beyond, focus on preservation: underground shelters, seed and DNA banks, and the bare blueprint of civilization.
- The smart money is on preparing for 3°C, bracing for 4°C, and localizing, fortifying, and adapting now.
