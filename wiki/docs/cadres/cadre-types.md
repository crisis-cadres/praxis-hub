# Cadre Types

- Cadres come in different shapes depending on their emphasis and level of commitment.
- [Procurement heavy](../procurement/index.md): a buying club or buyer's co-op that [buys goods in bulk](../strategy/bulk-buy.md) and distributes them to members at a discount.
- [Security only](../security/index.md): a neighborhood watch, marksmanship club, or self-defense class that meets to improve member skills.
- [Production heavy](../production/index.md): a community garden with a prominent horticulture sub-committee.
- Shared Nothing: a cadre that shares no physical resources, a [low-commitment starting point](crisis-continuum.md) like a call tree or social club.
- Fully Employed: all members work for the cadre full-time in lieu of other employment.
- Full Employment Seeking: a transitional state working toward full employment.
- [Full Housing (Seeking)](../strategy/housing.md): a cadre where everyone lives in cadre-provided housing, operating like a housing cooperative or land trust.
