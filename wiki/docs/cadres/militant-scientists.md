# Militant Scientists

- "Militant" signifies an unwavering commitment to reality, not aggression.
- People spread falsehoods and conspiracies because professing belief serves a social function, not because they believe; fact-checking alone fails.<sup>[\[1\]](#cite-1)</sup><sup>[\[3\]](#cite-3)</sup>
- Communities that incentivize false witnesses sever their connection to reality, with consequences that compound in crisis.<sup>[\[1\]](#cite-1)</sup><sup>[\[3\]](#cite-3)</sup>
- False witnesses are a case of costly signaling in humans: the professed belief is a signal of group loyalty, not a claim about the world.<sup>[\[2\]](#cite-2)</sup>
- Militant Science is a union of rigor and resilience: evidence is sovereign and irrationality is excluded.
- Evidence hierarchy: peer-reviewed data first, then empirical observation, then provisional expert consensus; speculation and tradition carry little weight.
- Members may hold personal beliefs, but all cadre-level decisions must be based on testable, evidence-based principles.
- A cadre that is a monoculture of thought is scientifically weak; [diversity of perspective](../culture/diversity.md) is required for rigor.
