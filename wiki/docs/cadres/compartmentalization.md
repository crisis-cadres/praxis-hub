# Political Compartmentalization

- Successful resilience requires compartmentalizing divisive political framing while the work of building self-sufficient communities remains deeply political.
- Culture-war debates, partisan noise, and irrelevant historical disputes drain time and energy and fracture unity.
- The guiding question for any discussion: does this directly inform a practical resilience strategy?
- Build, don't grandstand: address injustice by building systems so robust they render old inequalities obsolete, not by perfecting rhetoric.
- Implement discipline by delegating news monitoring, avoiding clout chasing, and neutralizing disruption tactics.
- When in doubt, focus on building; without a foundation of survival, there is no ground left on which to fight.
