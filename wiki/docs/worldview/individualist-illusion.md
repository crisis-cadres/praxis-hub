# The Individualist Illusion

- Lone-wolf prepping is a symptom of the competitive alienation and atomization that contribute to systemic fragility.
- Prepping has become a commodified anxiety, marketed through influencers, gear companies, and dystopian fiction.
- No individual or single family can possess all the skills or stockpile enough resources to endure a prolonged collapse alone.
- An isolated, well-stocked haven becomes an attractive target for desperate, organized groups.
- Prolonged isolation under duress has severe psychological consequences.
- History demonstrates that survival through crisis is a product of [collective action and mutual aid](../philosophy/cooperation.md).
- Real endurance is found in the strength of one's community, not the depth of one's bunker.
