# Worldview

- Resilience rests on durable, evidence-based worldviews rather than nationalist myths or religious dogma.
- Organize human communities in harmony with the land: [watersheds, soils, climate, and ecosystems rather than arbitrary political borders](bioregional-design.md).
- [Imperial collapse](when-empires-fall.md) redistributes suffering and opportunity unevenly, opening space for prepared, principled groups to build more equitable successors.
- Community and mutual aid, not [individual prepping](individualist-illusion.md), are the historically proven paths to survival.
