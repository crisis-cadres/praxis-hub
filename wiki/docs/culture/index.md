# Culture

- A cadre is a living culture, not just a structure: shared values, deliberate norms, and rituals that hold the group together between crises.
- [Political compartmentalization](compartmentalization.md) keeps the group focused on practical survival instead of divisive partisan debates.
- [Dunbar's number](dunbars-number.md) keeps the group at human scale so trust and cohesion stay real.
- [Diversity](diversity.md) is a strict strategic imperative that prevents groupthink and strengthens problem-solving.
- [Bioregional design](bioregional-design.md) roots identity in watersheds and ecosystems rather than arbitrary borders.
- Sharing a [surplus](surplus-recruitment.md) makes the group's values visible to the wider community.
