# Crisis Cadres

## From shared anxiety into shared action.

Crisis Cadres is an open-source playbook for community resilience. We take a realistic, pessimistic view of climate and societal collapse and turn it into pragmatic, local action. Small, committed groups called cadres build self-sufficiency through committees focused on production, procurement, education, and security.

## [Principles](philosophy/index.md)

- Societal and climate collapse are already underway, so organized, forward-looking groups must engineer community resilience against scarcity and disaster.
- A [realistic, pessimistic outlook](philosophy/realistic-pessimism.md) on the environment should motivate pragmatic, [collective action](philosophy/cooperation.md) rather than despair or ineffective symbolic gestures.
- [Political compartmentalization](culture/compartmentalization.md) keeps groups focused on practical survival strategies instead of divisive partisan debates.
- An absolute [commitment to reality and evidence-based decision-making](cadres/militant-scientists.md) is necessary to combat irrationality and conspiratorial thinking.
- Sub-topics: [From Pessimism to Action](philosophy/pessimism.md), [A Realistic Pessimistic Outlook](philosophy/realistic-pessimism.md), [Existential Dread](philosophy/existential-dread.md), [Cooperation Over Isolation](philosophy/cooperation.md), [The Individualist Illusion](philosophy/individualist-illusion.md), and [When Empires Fall](philosophy/when-empires-fall.md).

## [Crisis Cadres](cadres/index.md)

- [Crisis Cadres are organizations](cadres/what-is-a-cadre.md) of like-minded individuals collectively focused on providing resilience against whatever form the next crisis takes.
- They [counteract the isolation](philosophy/cooperation.md) and distrust bred by scarcity and uncertainty, fostering local self-sufficiency as a buffer against systemic failure.
- [Cadres exist on a continuum](cadres/crisis-continuum.md), from low-commitment social organizing to fully committed survival groups.
- Key disciplines include [political compartmentalization](culture/compartmentalization.md) and an uncompromising commitment to reality ([Militant Science](cadres/militant-scientists.md)).
- Producing a [surplus to share with the wider community](culture/surplus-recruitment.md) is the core recruitment and outreach strategy.

## [The Four Pillars](pillars/index.md)

- The Four Pillars are the core committees: [Production](production/index.md), [Procurement](procurement/index.md), [Education](education/index.md), and [Security](security/index.md).
- Committee-based governance brings task specialization and decentralized decision-making without bureaucratic inertia.
- Production creates essential goods, Procurement manages resource flows, Education cultivates skills, and Security protects the endeavor.
- When approaching a new problem, repeat the mantra: "Have we considered everything through the lens of Production, Procurement, Education, and Security?"
- The Pillars are interdependent components of a dynamic system, not rigid silos; [cadres](cadres/index.md) can mix and match and need not adopt all four.

## [Procurement](procurement/index.md)

- Procurement is the circulatory system of the community, managing the flow of material, financial, and informational resources.
- It scales along the continuum from "what does everyone want for lunch" to full-time property, fund, accounting, and legal management.
- Core duties include buying plans, purchasing, taxes, supplier management, inventory tracking, budgeting, compliance, logistics, and resource sharing.
- [The audit](procurement/audit.md) is a core function that shifts Procurement from a passive buyer into an architect of resilience.
- [Financial uplifting](procurement/financial-uplifting.md) and [enhanced financial strategies](procurement/financial-strategies.md) build a cadre wealth fund while raising the security of its most vulnerable members.
- Sub-topics: Procurement Fundamentals, [The Audit](procurement/audit.md), [Financial Uplifting](procurement/financial-uplifting.md), and [Enhanced Financial Strategies](procurement/financial-strategies.md).

## [Production](production/index.md)

- Production turns raw materials, labor, and knowledge into the essentials of life: food, water, shelter, energy, tools, and medicine.
- A community that cannot produce is perpetually one crisis away from collapse.
- "Appropriate Production" means making what is needed, with what is available, without waste, prioritizing low-energy, small-scale output.
- Production exists on a continuum, from mending clothes and growing herbs to a full-employment, self-sustaining economy.
- A cadre that produces a [surplus to share transforms resilience into recruitment](culture/surplus-recruitment.md) and economic leverage.
- Sub-topics: Production Fundamentals, [Food Production and Security](production/food-security.md), [Equipment Fabrication and Restoration](production/equipment.md), [Energy and Utilities](production/energy.md), [Medicine and Hygiene](production/medicine-hygiene.md), [Automation](production/automation.md), and [The Resilience Premium](production/resilience-premium.md).

## [Education](education/index.md)

- Education is the cadre's living mind: it asks what the group must know and ensures critical skills are deeply embedded, widely shared, and constantly evolving.
- Core responsibilities include knowledge preservation, conflict resolution, outreach, media relations, and [credentialing](education/credentialing.md).
- Education identifies skill gaps and designs tiered programs to fill them, in partnership with [Security](security/index.md) for critical skills.
- Cadres should cultivate a reputation as extremely learned and experienced: able to suture wounds, plant corn, mediate conflict, and balance books.
- [Media relations](education/media-narratives.md) default to silence, and the cadre's story is told on its own terms through controlled channels.
- Sub-topics: [Education Fundamentals](education/fundamentals.md), [Media Narratives and Information Sovereignty](education/media-narratives.md), and [Essential Skills and Credentialing](education/credentialing.md).

## [Security](security/index.md)

- Security graduates from basic preparedness to a professionalized community defense model as circumstances require.
- It starts with mundane foundations: call trees, neighborhood watch, site hardening, and pest control.
- Core duties include asset protection, [OPSEC](security/opsec.md), self-defense training, and operational discipline.
- The emphasis is on deterrence and defense, never offensive force; cadres are not expeditionary raiding forces.
- Advanced cadres study modern conflicts and harden against real threats while fostering a culture of confident preparedness.
- Sub-topics: Security Fundamentals, [Defensive Strategy](security/defensive-strategy.md), [Weapons and Legal Defense](security/weapons-legal.md), [The Corporate Edge](security/corporate-edge.md), and [OPSEC and Communication Resilience](security/opsec.md).

## [Beyond the Core Four](beyond-core-four/index.md)

- An anti-fragile posture requires specialized committees tailored to a community's unique circumstances and evolving needs.
- Common committee areas include Health and Wellness, [Housing and Infrastructure](strategy/housing.md), Transportation and Logistics, Communication and Technology, Ecology and Land Stewardship, Hospitality and Integration, and Scavenger and Salvage.
- Committee models differ by function: investigative, special purpose and spin-off, empowered, limited-function, and hybrid.
- New committees must form democratically from identified needs, pilot temporarily, and rotate roles to prevent burnout.
- Learn from local disasters to pinpoint gaps, and let members contribute to multiple committees based on capacity.
- Resilience is a living experiment: start with the [Four Pillars](pillars/index.md), stay flexible, and let necessity guide innovation.

## [Strategy](strategy/index.md)

- Early organizing should begin with simple, low-risk collective projects, such as a [bulk staple food purchase](strategy/bulk-buy.md), to build trust and demonstrate material benefits.
- [Starting a cadre](cadres/what-is-a-cadre.md) means naming the concrete problem, finding a few committed people, and building trust through small wins.
- [Early activities and shared concerns](strategy/early-activities.md) focus on the basics: enough food, clean water, and support for the most vulnerable.
- [Housing](strategy/housing.md) is the cornerstone of personal and collective security, secured through phased communal acquisition.
- A [community garden](strategy/horticulture.md) is a low-risk, high-visibility project that feeds members and demonstrates the cadre model.

## [Culture](culture/index.md)

- A cadre is a living culture, not just a structure: shared values, deliberate norms, and rituals that hold the group together between crises.
- [Political compartmentalization](culture/compartmentalization.md) keeps the group focused on practical survival instead of divisive partisan debates.
- [Dunbar's number](culture/dunbars-number.md) keeps the group at human scale so trust and cohesion stay real.
- [Diversity](culture/diversity.md) is a strict strategic imperative that prevents groupthink and strengthens problem-solving.
- [Bioregional design](culture/bioregional-design.md) roots identity in watersheds and ecosystems rather than arbitrary borders.
- Sharing a [surplus](culture/surplus-recruitment.md) makes the group's values visible to the wider community.

## [Governance](governance/index.md)

- Governance must be both participatory and decisive, transparent and efficient, avoiding the extremes of bureaucracy and autocracy.
- Representative government fails through corruption, and pure consensus fails through endless process; cadres strike a balance.
- Use [committee-based representatives](pillars/index.md) while keeping the group under [Dunbar's number](culture/dunbars-number.md) for agility.
- [Sortition, recallability](governance/framework.md), [Robert's Rules](appendices/roberts-rules.md), and [bylaws](governance/bylaws.md) prevent the concentration of power.
- [Subsidiarity](governance/subsidiarity.md) and [democratic centralism](governance/framework.md) keep decisions local yet unified once made.
- [Adding members](governance/adding-members.md) is governed by sponsorship, vetting, voting thresholds, and probation, with removal easier than admission.
