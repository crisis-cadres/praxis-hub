# Crisis Cadres

## [The Core Philosophy](core-philosophy.md)

* Societal and climate collapse are inevitable, requiring organized, forward-looking groups to engineer community resilience against scarcity and disaster.
* A realistic, pessimistic outlook on the environment should be used to motivate pragmatic, collective action rather than despair or climate idealism.
* Groups must practice political compartmentalization by intentionally avoiding divisive partisan debates and focusing strictly on practical survival strategies.
* An absolute commitment to reality and evidence-based decision-making is necessary to combat irrationality, superstition, and conspiratorial thinking.

## [The Four Pillars of Operations](four-pillars.md)

* **Production:** This core committee focuses on creating essential, life-sustaining goods like food, shelter, and energy to eliminate reliance on fragile external systems.
* **Procurement:** This committee manages the logistical flow of resources, handles finances, and oversees strategic stockpiling and equitable distribution.
* **Education:** This committee is responsible for internal knowledge transfer, conflict resolution, mental health support, and community outreach.
* **Security:** This committee protects community assets, manages operational security, and ensures confident, defensive preparedness without utilizing offensive aggression.

## [Organizational Strategy and Growth](organizational-strategy.md)

* Early organizing should begin with simple, low-risk collective projects, such as a bulk staple food purchase, to build initial trust and demonstrate immediate material benefits.
* Groups must operate within Dunbar's Number, keeping functional units small to maintain deep social trust and avoid bureaucratic stagnation.
* When groups grow beyond human-scale cognitive limits, they should scale outward through federated networks rather than establishing centralized bureaucracies.
* Demographic and cognitive diversity is a strict strategic imperative utilized to prevent groupthink, reduce vulnerabilities, and enhance problem-solving capabilities.

## [Tactics and Governance](tactics-governance.md)

* Organizing under the legal guise of an unremarkable small business provides strategic cover and operational durability during periods of societal decline.
* Addressing the housing crisis through collective property acquisition and shared communal living spaces is a primary method for securing member stability and reducing recurring costs.
* Group governance should utilize clear frameworks like Robert's Rules, rotating committee roles, and easily recallable representatives to prevent the centralization of power.
* Decisions should be localized to those most directly affected by an issue, while maintaining unified action across the group once a democratic decision is finalized.
