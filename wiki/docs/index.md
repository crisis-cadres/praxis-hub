# Crisis Cadres

## [Principles](philosophy/index.md)

- Societal and climate collapse are already underway, so organized, forward-looking groups must engineer community resilience against scarcity and disaster.
- A [realistic, pessimistic outlook](philosophy/realistic-pessimism.md) on the environment should motivate pragmatic, [collective action](philosophy/cooperation.md) rather than despair or [climate idealism](philosophy/climate-idealism.md).
- [Political compartmentalization](strategy/compartmentalization.md) keeps groups focused on practical survival strategies instead of divisive partisan debates.
- An absolute [commitment to reality and evidence-based decision-making](cadres/militant-scientists.md) is necessary to combat irrationality and conspiratorial thinking.
- Sub-topics: [From Pessimism to Action](philosophy/pessimism.md), [A Realistic Pessimistic Outlook](philosophy/realistic-pessimism.md), [Climate Idealism](philosophy/climate-idealism.md), [Existential Dread](philosophy/existential-dread.md), [Cooperation Over Isolation](philosophy/cooperation.md), [The Individualist Illusion](philosophy/individualist-illusion.md), and [When Empires Fall](philosophy/when-empires-fall.md).

## [Crisis Cadres](cadres/index.md)

- [Crisis Cadres are organizations](cadres/what-is-a-cadre.md) of like-minded individuals collectively focused on providing resilience against whatever form the next crisis takes.
- They [counteract the isolation](philosophy/cooperation.md) and distrust bred by scarcity and uncertainty, fostering local self-sufficiency as a buffer against systemic failure.
- [Cadres exist on a continuum](cadres/crisis-continuum.md), from low-commitment social organizing to fully committed survival groups.
- Key disciplines include [political compartmentalization](strategy/compartmentalization.md) and an uncompromising commitment to reality ([Militant Science](cadres/militant-scientists.md)).
- Producing a [surplus to share with the wider community](strategy/surplus-recruitment.md) is the core recruitment and outreach strategy.

## [The Four Pillars](pillars/index.md)

- The Four Pillars are the core committees: [Production](pillars/production/index.md), [Procurement](pillars/procurement/index.md), [Education](pillars/education/index.md), and [Security](pillars/security/index.md).
- Committee-based governance brings task specialization and decentralized decision-making without bureaucratic inertia.
- Production creates essential goods, Procurement manages resource flows, Education cultivates skills, and Security protects the endeavor.
- When approaching a new problem, repeat the mantra: "Have we considered everything through the lens of Production, Procurement, Education, and Security?"
- The Pillars are interdependent components of a dynamic system, not rigid silos; [cadres](cadres/index.md) can mix and match and need not adopt all four.

## [Strategy](strategy/index.md)

- Early organizing should begin with simple, low-risk collective projects, such as a [bulk staple food purchase](strategy/bulk-buy.md), to build trust and demonstrate material benefits.
- Groups must operate within [Dunbar's Number](strategy/dunbars-number.md), keeping functional units small to preserve deep social trust.
- When groups grow beyond human-scale cognitive limits, they should scale through [federation](strategy/federation.md) rather than centralized bureaucracy.
- [Diversity](strategy/diversity.md) is a strict strategic imperative that prevents groupthink and enhances problem-solving.
- [Housing](strategy/housing.md) is the cornerstone of personal and collective security, secured through phased communal acquisition.
- [Bioregional design](strategy/bioregional-design.md) organizes communities along watersheds and ecosystems rather than arbitrary borders.
- [Political compartmentalization](strategy/compartmentalization.md) keeps groups focused on practical survival strategies instead of divisive partisan debates.
- Producing a [surplus to share with the wider community](strategy/surplus-recruitment.md) is the core recruitment and outreach strategy.
- [Starting a cadre](cadres/what-is-a-cadre.md) means naming the concrete problem, finding a few committed people, and building trust through small wins.

## [Governance](governance/index.md)

- Governance must be both participatory and decisive, transparent and efficient, avoiding the extremes of bureaucracy and autocracy.
- Representative government fails through corruption, and pure consensus fails through endless process; cadres strike a balance.
- Use [committee-based representatives](pillars/index.md) while keeping the group under [Dunbar's number](strategy/dunbars-number.md) for agility.
- [Sortition, recallability](governance/framework.md), [Robert's Rules](appendices/roberts-rules.md), and [bylaws](governance/bylaws.md) prevent the concentration of power.
- [Subsidiarity](governance/subsidiarity.md) and [democratic centralism](governance/framework.md) keep decisions local yet unified once made.
- [Adding members](governance/adding-members.md) is governed by sponsorship, vetting, voting thresholds, and probation, with removal easier than admission.

## From shared anxiety into shared action

- [Praxis Hub](https://github.com/crisis-cadres/praxis-hub) is the open-source home of the Crisis Cadres wiki, operated on GitHub as a living document.
- The wiki contains well-researched hypotheses to be tested, debated, and improved upon by those serious about doing the work.
- Anyone can contribute: fix typos, clarify sentences, correct errors, or suggest entirely new sections via pull requests.
- Field reports and case studies are especially valuable: tell us what worked and what failed spectacularly.
