# Code of Conduct

Praxis Hub is a living document meant to be tested, debated, and improved by
people serious about doing the work. This Code of Conduct keeps collaboration
disciplined and mission-focused: it exists to protect the work, not to police
personalities.

## Our Standards

Contributions — issues, discussions, and pull requests — should be:

- **Evidence-based.** Claims should be grounded in reality and open to
  correction. When you are wrong, update.
- **Mission-focused.** Ask the guiding question before you post: does this
  directly inform a practical resilience strategy? If not, it does not belong
  in the discussion.
- **Compartimentalized.** Keep culture-war debates, partisan noise, and
  irrelevant historical disputes out. The work of building self-sufficient
  communities remains deeply political; the framing does not need to be
  partisan.
- **Constructive.** Build, don't grandstand. Suggest concrete improvements:
  fix typos, clarify sentences, correct errors, or propose new sections.
- **Respectful.** No personal attacks, no ad hominem, no harassment. Attack
  ideas, not people.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Personal attacks, insults, or harassment of any kind.
- Partisan grandstanding, culture-war arguments, or trolling.
- Spreading misinformation or knowingly defending falsehoods after correction.
- Clout chasing: promoting yourself, a brand, or an unrelated agenda over the work.
- Any behavior that discourages others from contributing.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.
They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and other
contributions that violate it, and may ban a contributor temporarily or
permanently when the behavior warrants it. Reports are handled promptly and,
when possible, privately. Retaliation against a person who reports a violation
is itself a violation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all Praxis Hub spaces — issues, pull
requests, discussions, and wiki edits — and to public representation of the
project.

## Reporting

To report a violation, open a discussion via the
[Start a discussion](https://github.com/crisis-cadres/praxis-hub/issues/new)
link on any page, or contact the maintainers directly through GitHub.
All reports are taken seriously.
